# SmartERP

A modern cloud-based **Billing, Inventory & Accounting Management System** inspired by **TallyPrime**, developed using modern web technologies.

---

# Project Overview

SmartERP is designed to help businesses manage:

- Accounting
- Inventory
- Billing
- Sales
- Purchases
- GST
- Customer Management
- Supplier Management
- Financial Reports

The system follows a **keyboard-first workflow**, allowing accountants and operators to perform all major operations without relying on a mouse.

---

# Project Objectives

- Multi-company management
- Keyboard-only navigation
- Customer & Supplier Ledger
- Inventory Management
- Purchase Voucher
- Sales Voucher
- GST Billing
- Reports
- Invoice Generation
